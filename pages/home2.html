🌈 Tiny Artists (Ages 4–6)

The youngest creators explore the magical world of colour, texture, and shape through guided sensory play and simple art activities. Every session is designed to be mess-friendly and confidence-building.

  • ✓ Finger & sponge painting
  • ✓ Colour mixing exploration
  • ✓ Paper collage & tearing
  • ✓ Seasonal art projects
  • ✓ Story-inspired art making

🖌️ Junior Artists (Ages 7–9)

Junior Artists dive into more structured art-making — learning to observe, plan, and create with growing confidence. Projects grow in complexity while remaining full of discovery and fun.

  • ✓ Basic drawing & shading
  • ✓ Watercolour techniques
  • ✓ Poster art & mixed media
  • ✓ Nature observation drawings
  • ✓ Simple perspective basics

🎭 Studio Artists (Ages 10–12)

Studio Artists work with a wider range of materials and techniques, developing a genuine artistic vocabulary and personal style. Projects culminate in a termly showcase exhibition.

  • ✓ Acrylic & oil pastel painting
  • ✓ Colour theory & composition
  • ✓ Canvas painting projects
  • ✓ Sketchbook journaling
  • ✓ Art history inspiration

🏆 Advanced Artists (Ages 13–16)

Advanced Artists develop serious skills for academic and portfolio purposes. Sessions are studio-style, with individual project direction and peer critique to build artistic maturity.

  • ✓ Portfolio development
  • ✓ Perspective & life drawing
  • ✓ Mixed media exploration
  • ✓ Portrait & figure art
