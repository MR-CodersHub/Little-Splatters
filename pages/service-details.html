Class Structure

What Happens Each Session

Every 60-minute Watercolour Wonder session follows a thoughtful, child-centred structure that balances skill learning with creative freedom.

1

Welcome & Warm-Up (10 min)

Children settle in, collect materials, and complete a quick warm-up exercise — colour mixing, brush drills, or a fun mark-making game. Sets a calm, creative mood.

2

Technique Introduction (15 min)

Instructor demonstrates the session's focus technique — wet-on-wet, resist, layering, etc. — with a mini demonstration painting that excites and inspires.

3

Guided Project (25 min)

The heart of the session — children apply the technique to their own creative project. Instructor circulates, offering individual encouragement and guidance.

4

Share & Celebrate (10 min)

The group gathers to share work, give positive observations, and talk about what they discovered. Artwork is displayed for collection. Pure pride all round!

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about Watercolour Wonder — and any of our classes.

Does my child need any watercolour experience?
+
Absolutely not! Watercolour Wonder welcomes complete beginners and experienced young painters alike. Our instructors assess each child's level in the first session and pace lessons individually. No prior experience is needed — just curiosity and enthusiasm!
What do we need to bring?
+
Nothing at all! All materials — paints, brushes, paper, palettes, and aprons — are provided. We just ask that children wear clothing that can get a little paint on it, or bring a change of top. A water bottle is always welcome.
What happens if we miss a class?
+
We understand that life happens! Monthly members can carry over one missed class per month with 24 hours' notice. We also offer catch-up sessions on Saturday mornings for missed weekday classes where spaces allow.
Can parents stay during class?
+
For children aged 4–6 a parent or carer is welcome to stay. For children 7 and over, we gently encourage drop-off — children tend to engage more freely and confidently without a parent present. We have a comfortable waiting area and send photos of the session!
How do I book a free trial class?
+
Simply fill in our trial class booking form on the Contact page — or call us on +1 (555) 287-ARTS. We'll confirm availability within 24 hours and send you all the details. Your first trial class is completely free and no commitment is required.
Do children get to keep their artwork?
+
Absolutely — every piece belongs to your child! We keep work to dry safely after each class and it's ready for collection by the following session. We also take digital photos of every piece for our parent portal and with permission, our social media showcase.
