Privacy Policy

Your family's trust matters most. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and how you stay in control.

Effective: Jan 1, 2026 Last updated: Oct 8, 2026
On this page 1. What we collect 2. How we use it 3. Children's privacy 4. Sharing & storage 5. Cookies 6. Your rights 7. Contact us

Short version: we collect only what we need to run classes safely (contact details, enrolment info, messages you send us), never sell your data, and you can ask us to show, fix or delete it anytime.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details — parent/guardian name, email, phone, and authorised pick-up contacts.
  • Enrolment details — child's first name, age, class preferences, medical/allergy or support needs relevant to safe participation.
  • Messages — trial bookings, contact forms, emails and waitlist sign-ups.
  • Website data — basic analytics (pages visited, device type) and preferences like theme (light/dark) stored locally in your browser.

We do not collect payment card numbers on our servers — payments are processed by our secure payment provider.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm trials, manage enrolments, run classes and communicate schedule changes.
  • To keep children safe (allergies, pick-up lists, emergency contacts).
  • To send class updates and — only with your opt-in — news and art tips. Unsubscribe anytime with one click.
  • To improve our website and studio experience using aggregated, anonymous statistics.

3. Children's privacy

We take extra care with children's information. We collect the minimum needed for safe participation, only from a parent or legal guardian, and never for advertising. Photos or artwork featuring a child are only shared publicly with explicit written parental consent, which can be withdrawn at any time — just email us.

4. Sharing & storage

  • We never sell personal data. We share it only with essential service providers (e.g. email delivery, secure hosting, payment processing) under strict confidentiality.
  • Data is stored securely with access limited to authorised staff. Enrolment records are kept for as long as needed for safety, legal and tax purposes, then securely deleted or anonymised.
  • If legally required (e.g. child safety concern), we may disclose information to the appropriate authorities.

6. Your rights

  • Access — ask for a copy of the data we hold about you and your child.
  • Correct — ask us to fix anything inaccurate.
  • Delete — ask us to delete data we no longer need to keep for legal or safety reasons.
  • Withdraw consent — opt out of photos, marketing emails or waitlist messages anytime.

To exercise any right, email hello@littlesplatters.com. We respond within 30 days.
