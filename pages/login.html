Account Help

Frequently Asked Account Questions

Is creating a parent account mandatory?
+
An account is recommended for enrolled students as it gives you full access to your child's portfolio, progress reports, and class management. For trial classes, no account is needed — just fill in our booking form on the Contact page.
Can I add multiple children to one account?
+
Absolutely! One parent account can manage multiple children. Each child has their own profile, portfolio, and class schedule within your single account. Billing for multiple children is consolidated for your convenience.
What happens to my account if I cancel my membership?
+
Your account and your child's digital portfolio remain accessible even after a membership ends. You can re-enrol at any time. We never delete student artwork or progress records without explicit request.
How is my data protected?
+
We take data protection extremely seriously. All personal data is encrypted, stored securely, and never shared with third parties. We are fully GDPR compliant and our Privacy Policy outlines all data handling practices in plain language.
