FAQ

Common Questions Answered

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from new families joining Little Splatters.

How does the free trial class work?
+
Book via our form above. We'll confirm your slot within 24 hours. Your child attends a full class for free — all materials provided. After the trial, you can enrol if you love it — no pressure whatsoever.
What age groups do you teach?
+
We welcome children aged 4–16. Our classes are grouped by age: Tiny Artists (4–6), Junior Artists (7–9), Studio Artists (10–12), and Advanced Artists (13–16). Each group has age-appropriate curriculum, materials, and class length.
Do you offer classes for children with special needs?
+
Absolutely. We are inclusive and welcome all children. We ask that parents inform us of any specific needs in the booking form so we can plan accordingly. Many of our instructors have experience with sensory-processing differences, ADHD, and autism spectrum.
What if my child doesn't enjoy their trial class?
+
That's completely okay! We'd love to understand why and see if a different class or time might suit better. There is absolutely zero obligation to enrol after a trial — we only want children who are genuinely excited to be here.
Is parking available at the studio?
+
Yes! We have a dedicated car park for 20 vehicles directly adjacent to the studio entrance. Additional street parking is available on Paintbrush Lane and the surrounding streets.
Can my child join mid-term?
+
Yes — we accept enrolments at any point in the term. The monthly membership rate is pro-rated for mid-term joiners. Our classes are structured to welcome new students at any time without disrupting the group.
Do you offer birthday party sessions?
+
Yes! Our private birthday art parties are incredibly popular. We host groups of 8–16 children for a themed 90-minute art session in our studio. Contact us for availability and package pricing.
How do I pay for classes?
+
We accept payment by credit/debit card, bank transfer, or cash. Monthly memberships are billed automatically at the start of each month. Single class passes can be purchased in advance or at the door.
