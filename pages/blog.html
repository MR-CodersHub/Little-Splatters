Pro Tips

Drawing & Painting Tips from Our Instructors

Quick, actionable tips from our expert instructors to help your child improve faster and enjoy art even more.

Watercolour Tips

1
Always wet your brush first

A damp brush picks up pigment evenly and prevents harsh, scratchy strokes.

2
Let layers dry completely

Patience creates clean, luminous layers. Use a hairdryer on cool setting to speed things up.

3
Mix colours on the palette

Always test mixes on a scrap before applying to your painting. Saves frustration!

Drawing Tips

1
Draw lightly at first

Light pencil marks can be erased; dark ones leave dents. Build up pressure gradually.

2
Start with basic shapes

Everything can be broken into circles, squares, and triangles. Start simple, then add detail.

3
Draw what you SEE, not what you know

Look closely at the actual shapes of light and shadow — not the idea of the object.

Crafts Tips

1
Collect a material "bank"

Save cardboard tubes, fabric scraps, buttons, bottle caps — treasure for craft projects!

2
Protect surfaces first

A wipe-clean tablecloth or newspaper sheet transforms messy craft into stress-free fun.

Colour Theory Tips

1
Limit your palette

Fewer colours = more harmony. Start with 3 and mix your way to 30!

2
Use opposite colours for vibrancy

Complementary colours (blue/orange, red/green) make each other pop when placed side by side.
