Terms of Service

Welcome to Little Splatters! By booking a trial, enrolling your child, or using this website, you agree to these Terms. If anything is unclear, please ask us — we're happy to explain.

1. Enrolment & Free Trial

  • Children aged 4–16 may enrol. Classes are grouped by age and skill so every child is supported.
  • The first trial class is free, with all supplies included. No credit card is required to book a trial.
  • Enrolment is confirmed once we send written confirmation (email or SMS) and payment is received where applicable.
  • Parents/guardians must provide accurate contact, medical and allergy information at enrolment and keep it updated.

2. Payments & Refunds

  • Pricing is published on our Pricing page. No hidden fees.
  • Monthly memberships renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime with 14 days' notice before the next billing date.
  • Single workshops and camps are paid in full at booking.
  • Refunds: full refund if we cancel a class; pro-rata credit if your child cannot continue for medical reasons (with documentation). Change-of-mind refunds are available within 48 hours of purchase if no class has been attended.

3. Cancellations & Missed Classes

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule. One make-up class per term is guaranteed where space allows.
  • If we cancel (e.g. instructor illness, weather), we offer a make-up session or account credit — your choice.
  • Late pick-ups (over 15 minutes) may incur a supervision fee. Please call if you're running late.

4. Studio Conduct

  • We celebrate kindness, curiosity and creative courage. Bullying, unkind language or unsafe behaviour is addressed gently but firmly, in partnership with parents.
  • Children should wear art-friendly clothes. We provide aprons, but paint happens — that's the fun!
  • All materials are non-toxic and child-safe. Please tell us about allergies, sensory needs or additional support requirements.

5. Safeguarding & Safety

  • All instructors are background-checked and trained in child safeguarding and first aid.
  • Children are supervised at all times and released only to authorised adults listed at enrolment.
  • We maintain a strict sign-in / sign-out register. Please notify us in writing of any change to pick-up arrangements.
  • If you have a safeguarding concern, contact us immediately at hello@littlesplatters.com or +1 (555) 287-ARTS.

6. Photos & Artwork

  • We love celebrating student work. We only photograph or share your child's artwork or image with your written opt-in consent, which you can withdraw anytime.
  • Original artwork belongs to the child. Digital portfolios remain accessible via the parent portal during active enrolment.

7. Liability

We take every reasonable care to provide a safe studio. To the maximum extent permitted by law, Little Splatters is not liable for loss or damage arising from participation except where caused by our negligence. Nothing in these Terms limits rights you have under applicable consumer law.

8. Contact & Changes

We may update these Terms occasionally. Material changes will be notified by email at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued enrolment after that date means you accept the updated Terms.
