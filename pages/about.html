Our Story

Born from a Love of Art
& a Love of Children

Little Splatters began in 2014 as a single classroom, a few tins of paint, and an unwavering belief that every child is a born creative. Today we're a thriving studio community for hundreds of young artists and their families.

Our Philosophy

We Teach Children to See, Feel & Express

Our approach blends structured skill-building with open-ended exploration — because the best art education gives children both a toolkit and the freedom to use it.

Child-Led Learning

Every project starts with a child's curiosity. We follow their interests, let them direct the narrative, and guide their execution with expert technique.

Process Over Product

We celebrate the artistic journey — the choosing, experimenting, and discovering — not just the finished piece. The process builds confidence and resilience.

Inclusive & Affirming

Every child's artistic voice is worthy. We create a culture where there are no "wrong" answers — only unique, valid expressions waiting to be discovered.

Skills That Last

Structured technique is woven throughout our programmes — children leave Little Splatters with real skills in colour, composition, and medium-specific craft.

World Art Traditions

We expose children to art from cultures worldwide — from Japanese ink painting to African textile patterns — building cultural appreciation and broad inspiration.

Courage to Create

We build brave creators — children who are willing to experiment, take risks, make mistakes, and keep going. These are life skills as much as art skills.

Artistic Growth

How We Help Children Grow as Artists

Our structured developmental approach ensures that children build genuine skills over time — with milestones that parents can see and children can feel proud of.

Month 1–2
Foundation & Discovery

Children learn basic materials, colour exploration, mark-making, and develop comfort in the creative environment.

Month 3–4
Skill Building

Structured techniques introduced — line work, shading, colour mixing, composition basics. Projects become more deliberate.

Month 5–6
Personal Style Emerges

Children begin showing their unique artistic voice. Projects become personal, expressive, and increasingly sophisticated.

Month 7+
Portfolio & Exhibition

Students compile a personal portfolio, participate in showcase exhibitions, and set artistic goals for the next term.

Termly Progress Reports

Parents receive detailed written feedback on their child's artistic development, skills gained, and areas of growing strength.

Digital Portfolio

Every student maintains a digital portfolio of their artwork, accessible to parents via our private parent portal.

Showcase Exhibitions

Twice-yearly family art exhibitions celebrate every student's work in a gallery-style event that builds real pride and confidence.

Our Values

The Principles That Guide Everything We Do

Our values aren't decorative — they shape every class, every interaction, and every creative decision we make.

01

Genuine Care

We genuinely care about every child as an individual — their confidence, growth, emotional wellbeing, and creative joy matter deeply to us.

02

Excellence in Teaching

We never compromise on the quality of our instruction. Every lesson is planned, purposeful, and delivered with skill and enthusiasm.

03

Inclusivity & Belonging

Every child — regardless of background, ability, or experience — belongs here and is welcomed, celebrated, and supported.

04

Safety First

Physical and emotional safety are non-negotiable. All materials are non-toxic, all instructors are background-checked, and our safeguarding policies are robust.

05

Growth Mindset

We celebrate effort, persistence, and creative risk-taking above outcomes. Mistakes are learning moments and every attempt has value.

06

Community Partnership

We partner closely with parents and see them as creative partners in their child's artistic development — communication is always open and warm.
